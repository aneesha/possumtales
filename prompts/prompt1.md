Create an interactive 3D picture book website where users select a picture book from a bookshelf, and upon opening it, three-dimensional characters and landscapes pop out.

Allow users to advance the story themselves by clicking, ensuring a seamless experience within a single space that connects the bookshelf, book movement, cover opening, page turning, and the story's conclusion.

【Experience Design】
・Start by arranging multiple picture books on the bookshelf, each with distinct cover illustrations and titles
・When a book is selected, it moves from the shelf to the desk in front, and the cover opens
・Characters, objects, and landscapes rise up three-dimensionally on the page
・First, create a short story for one book that can be fully experienced in 3–4 scenes
・Clicking on characters or objects triggers reactions that lead to the next event
・When turning a page, the 3D elements on that page shrink and temporarily disappear completely, with the next scene rising up only after the page turn is complete
・The camera zooms in close enough to capture character expressions and moves smoothly in accordance with the story
・Avoid screen cuts or sudden viewpoint shifts, connecting all scenes with continuous motion
・At the end, include a simple quiz tied to the story and a reward collection animation

【Design and Sound】
・Reflect the theme or worldview to be added after this prompt
・Use soft colors reminiscent of picture books, warm lighting, and paper textures
・Keep characters as the focal point, without obscuring their faces with text or operation buttons
・Use short, easy-to-read English for text and character dialogue
・Add a subtle trail of light to the mouse cursor that faintly lingers when moved
・Exclude read-aloud or voiced dialogue; compose with gentle BGM, small click sounds, and paper-turning effects
・Do not leave placeholder images, dummy text, or incomplete elements

【Implementation】
・Use Three.js or an appropriate WebGL technology
・Design characters with rounded, cute forms, and prepare 3D models or cover illustrations that fit the theme
・Synchronize user clicks with animations, ensuring that rapid clicking does not disrupt the scene sequence
・Enable mouse operation on PC and tap operation on smartphones
・Start sound playback after the user's first interaction, and include a mute option
・Create a complete file that can be launched locally
・Wait for model and image loading before starting the experience
・Verify no console errors, display glitches, or character clipping
